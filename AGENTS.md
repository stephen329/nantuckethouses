> agents before any content, SEO, or page change. Governed by the shared
> **[NETWORK.md](./NETWORK.md)** charter (canonical source: the `odin` repo at `docs/governance/NETWORK.md`; the copy beside this file is a read-only mirror) — that file wins on any conflict.

## This brand's one job

**Discover — the intelligence layer.** Nantucket Houses is the research surface
buyers and owners open *before* they're ready to call anyone. It captures the
research audience, qualifies them with data, and **hands them to Congdon & Coleman**
to transact. It does not close deals itself.

Positioning: *"20+ years of Nantucket judgment you can't Google."*
Voice: precise, confident, insider — data with a human read on it.

## SEO — what we own / what we must never touch

**OWN (informational / investigational intent):**
`nantucket market report` · `comparable / recent sales` · `nantucket zoning & buildability` ·
`what did [address] sell for` · `nantucket property records / deed history` ·
`nantucket real estate app` · Market Pulse topics.

**NEVER target:** "list with an agent", "sell my home", "book a rental", or any
transactional sales/rental query. Those belong to Coleman and Rentals.
**Never** headline the bare phrase "Nantucket real estate" — win it through depth.

## Required handoffs (mandatory, contextual)

- Every **Market Pulse** post and **listing page** MUST carry a CTA to Congdon &
  Coleman ("Ready to make a move? Work with the island's brokerage").
- Treat listings as **engagement, not "for sale" money pages** — do not compete for
  purchase-intent keywords; convert that intent by routing to Coleman.
- Link "about / the people behind this" to **maury.net** (the canonical founder bio) —
  do not re-host the full founder narrative here.

## Content role & cadence

The network's **demand engine** — highest publishing frequency.
Weekly Market Pulse · weekly "what sold" · monthly zoning/buildability & neighborhood
data deep-dives. Everything is top-of-funnel data storytelling that feeds Coleman.

## Canonicalization

- Listing/comps data is the **canonical source** for the network. If it appears on
  Coleman, Coleman embeds it and points `rel=canonical` here.
- The iOS app is the primary product; the website is its discovery/SEO companion —
  don't duplicate app-only flows as indexable pages that compete with the app.
